This is where Landon and Cass keep the workshops, the people, the trees and the supplies. Every page below is made for a phone: big buttons, one thing at a time.

What each page is for

  1. HomeThe short version of what's coming up.
  2. My workYour own to-do list and a countdown to the next workshop. Tap your name the first time.
  3. CalendarThe workshops on a month grid. Best on a computer.
  4. Workshop dayWho's coming and what to bring, on the morning of.
  5. Plan a workshopSet up a new date and its prep steps.
  6. BoardsWorkshops and trees in lanes. Move a card to change where it stands.
  7. Phone bookFind a person. Call, text or email in one tap.
  8. Workshop checklistPhotos, care sheets, surveys: tick each one once it's done.
  9. Kit and suppliesWhat's on the shelf and what to reorder.
  10. TreesWhose tree is whose, and when it's due for a trim.
  11. Sign-in sheetThe print-ready sheet and name tags.
  12. People, Season, Quick addThe bigger picture, and a fast way to add someone new.

Quick add

Met someone who wants a seat? Start here.

Done and Undo

  1. Tap Done on a task. It leaves your list.
  2. A small bar at the bottom says Undo for a few seconds.
  3. Missed it? Open Done lately at the bottom of My work and tap Undo there.

Ticking a box on a checklist works the same way: tap to tick, tap again to untick. A dashed box fills in by itself. Tap it and it tells you why.

If something goes wrong

You can't break it. Every change is kept in Document history (in the Grist menu), with who changed what and when, and you can undo from there.

Put it on your home screen

iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: open it in Chrome, tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen.

It opens like an app, with the Zion Bonsai logo.
