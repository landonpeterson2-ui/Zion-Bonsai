# Our Story — "It started during COVID"

Final, approved copy from Cass (2026-10-23). Use exactly as written. Page: /our-story, "How it started" section.

My husband, Landon, fell down a bonsai rabbit hole on YouTube — the kind where it’s suddenly 2am and you’re watching Peter Chan wire a juniper in the UK.

In 2021 he ordered his first tree from Amazon. Inspired by Gilbert Cantu of Little Jade Bonsai, he started with a dwarf jade, forgiving enough to grow indoors. One plant turned into two, then a small collection quietly taking over our back room.

The wiring, the ancient technique, the soil science? Information overload for me. I just wanted something pretty to look at, and I wasn’t too keen on the “rules.”

Then our youngest was born in 2022. Postpartum complications blindsided me, and some days all I could do was sit and watch Landon trim his trees.

Somewhere in that fog, I started sitting with them myself. Not to learn anything or get it right. Just to be near something quiet and alive. The trees didn’t ask anything of me. They kept growing, at their own pace, on their own terms. Slowly, so did I.

Bonsai taught me to give myself the same grace I gave those trees.

Landon brought the knowledge. I found the heart of it. And eventually I wondered: what if other people could have this too?

If you’re in a hard season of your own, there’s a seat for you at our table.
