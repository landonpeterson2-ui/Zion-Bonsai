## Sections
1. **Intro** — for past attendees and the curious. Reassuring: jades are forgiving.
2. **Watering, the one thing to get right** — water thoroughly until it drains completely out the bottom, then let it dry out. It's not about whether the soil *feels* dry. Bonsai hates wet feet — never let roots sit in water; jades store water in stems and leaves.
3. **The leaf snap test** — snap a leaf: firm and wet inside = hydrated, wait; limp and dry = time to water. Make this a visual moment (illustration or photo placeholder `[PHOTO — snapped jade leaf]`).
4. **Light & where to keep it** — dwarf jade (*Portulacaria*) and jade (*Crassula*) thrive indoors year-round in Central Utah — a real advantage over outdoor bonsai here.
5. **Seasons** — summer is the faster growth season; fall and winter are slower, **not dormant**, just paced.
6. **Growth stages** — a visual timeline from Plantlet up through multi-year established trees. Use stage names Landon provides; until then label the ends "Plantlet" and "Established" and mark middle stages `[STAGE NAME]`.
7. **Our soil** — custom mix that holds just enough moisture without staying soggy.
8. **When to come back** — return shaping sessions in 6–9 months → `/workshops`.
